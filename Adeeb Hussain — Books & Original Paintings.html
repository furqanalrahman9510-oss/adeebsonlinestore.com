About the Author & Artist

A desk, a drafting table, and a shelf of half-finished notebooks.

Adeeb Hussain works between two disciplines: writing that moves slowly and deliberately, and painting done in a single sitting. His books travel through memory, maps, and unfinished cities; his canvases hold a single hour of light.
